About All Together Now

All Together Now is a church membership database designed to help small to medium-sized churches better manage their congregation. It centralizes member and visitor information, simplifies attendance tracking, and provides ministry-specific analytics — giving church leaders a clear view of the health and growth of their community.

Our Purpose

Many churches struggle to keep track of members, visitors, and ministries using scattered records or manual systems. All Together Now was built to make this process simple, structured, and secure — enabling church administrators and ministry leaders to:

  • Add, edit, or remove member and visitor records easily.
  • Group members into ministries and manage leadership roles.
  • Track attendance for Sunday services and ministry meetings using QR codes.
  • Generate and view attendance analytics to monitor participation and growth.
  • Follow up with new visitors through connection cards routed to the appropriate ministry.

User Roles

Admin

Admins manage all members, visitors, and ministries. They can create ministry leader accounts, generate attendance forms and QR codes, and view all analytics for the church.

Ministry Leader

Ministry Leaders manage members within their specific ministry. They can take attendance, view ministry analytics, and receive visitor connection cards related to their group.

Visitor

Visitors can fill out a digital connection card using a QR code, optionally provide contact information, and receive follow-ups from ministry leaders based on their interests.

Our Mission

To bring clarity, connection, and community to church operations by providing a tool that helps leaders stay organized, connected to their members, and focused on ministry growth — all together now.
